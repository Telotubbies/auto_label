# research/

Provider-side material: reports and investigation notes that informed the product.
Nothing here is built, imported or shipped.

- `reports/` — research reports on competitors, UX and SaaS feature sets.
- `notes/` — working notes behind those reports.

The boundary with `docs/research/` is a subject boundary, not a format one:
`docs/research/` is *product* research that shapes the roadmap — the parity
backlog and competitive teardown that tasks are cut from — so it stays under
`docs/` next to the spec. `research/` here is provider-side material: surveys,
vendor comparisons and notes that are read while deciding, not while building.
