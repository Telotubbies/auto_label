## User story
As a DPO, I want faces, plates and a neighbour's window redacted on the way in, and an export that keeps the original only with an approval and a reason, so that a complaint is answered by a record of what we did rather than by a promise.

## UX flow (LabelDesk words)
1. **The camera carries its own policy** — `blur_plates`, `masks` and `export_policy` are set per stream; the ingest path applies them before the bytes are stored, exactly where faces are blurred today. [empty: a camera with no policy row behaves as today: faces only]
2. **A policy the box cannot honour** — `blur_plates` on with no plate detector installed makes the camera refuse frames: `เปิดเบลอป้ายทะเบียนไว้แต่ยังไม่ได้ติดตั้งตัวตรวจจับ — กล้องนี้ไม่รับภาพเข้าชุดข้อมูล`, instead of storing a clear plate. [error: that sentence, and the stream shows it]
3. **The preview** — `GET /{sid}/privacy-preview` returns two signed stills of the last frame, `redacted` and `original`, plus what was applied, so the operator sees what the public will see. [error: `ยังไม่มีภาพให้ตรวจ — เปิดกล้องก่อน`]
4. **The export policy** — `original` is refused with 403 unless the caller is a manager and supplies `export_reason`; the package that leaves carries the policy, the reason and the operator. [error: `ส่งออกต้นฉบับต้องมีสิทธิ์ผู้จัดการและเหตุผลที่บันทึกไว้`]
5. **One blur path** — ingest, video frames, the live preview and the evidence package all call `redact.apply`; there is no second implementation to forget.

## Backend changes
- Models/schema: `Stream` gains `blur_plates BOOLEAN DEFAULT FALSE`, `masks JSON DEFAULT []`, `export_policy VARCHAR(16) DEFAULT 'redacted'` — one `STREAMS` block in `app/schema/streams.py`; no new table.
- Routers: `routers/streams.py` — `StreamIn`/`StreamPatch` accept the three fields (masks ≤ 8 polygons, 3–200 points each, normalised), and one route `GET "/{sid}/privacy-preview"` → `{redacted, original, applied: {faces, plates, masks}}`; `PATCH` returns 400 for an unknown policy or an out-of-range mask. 403 for `export_policy: "original"` from a caller without `project.settings`.
- Services/jobs: `app/services/plateblur.py` (new) — `available()`, `detect_plates(gray, detector=None)`, `blur_plates(im, detector=None) -> (im, n)` with an injectable detector and no bundled model (the same optional-driver shape as `zxing-cpp` and `asyncua`); `app/services/redact.py` (new) — `available(project, stream) -> (bool, str)`, `apply(im, *, faces, plates, masks) -> (im, dict)` calling `faceblur.blur_faces`, `plateblur.blur_plates` and a solid fill for each mask polygon (masks are filled, never blurred). `routers/images.py::ingest` and `handlers/streams.py` switch their `faceblur.blur_faces` call to `redact.apply` and their availability check to `redact.available`. No job.
- Permissions + workspace isolation: reading and changing a camera's policy needs `project.privacy` (manager) to change and `project.streams` to read; the stream is resolved through `workspace_id` + `project_id`, so a foreign id is 404.
- Secrets/safefetch: `services/secretbox.py` — not needed (no new credential); `services/safefetch.py` — not called; the stills are our own signed paths under `DATA_DIR`, and anything outside it is dropped rather than served.
- OpenAPI: yes — one route, three stream fields and their validation.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Faces, plates and static masks are applied on ingest, on video frames and on the preview through one function, and masks are filled solid.
- [ ] A camera whose policy the box cannot honour refuses frames with the stated sentence instead of storing a clear plate.
- [ ] The export policy defaults to redacted, and `original` is refused without a manager and a recorded reason.
- [ ] Empty, loading and error states above all render — a camera with no frame previews as a sentence, not a broken image.
- [ ] Workspace A cannot read or change workspace B's camera policy or preview (404, not 403).
- [ ] Flag off ⇒ the three fields are rejected, no plate path runs, and ingest keeps blurring faces exactly as today.

## Notes for the implementer
R7: this is the product's anonymisation promise, so the failure direction is fixed — a missing plate detector, a corrupt cascade or an unreadable polygon all refuse the frame rather than store it clear, and `redact.apply` is the only place that decides. Stored and for how long: the plate detector is optional and ships no weights; the masks and the policy live on the stream row and are kept with it; the `privacy.original` audit row (who, when, which stream, the reason) is kept one year beyond the project's `retention_days`. Degrade to the safe state: a policy that cannot be honoured blocks the camera; an export that cannot be redacted is not produced.
