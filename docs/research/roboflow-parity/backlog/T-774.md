## User story
As the vendor manager at quarter end, I want the month's measured availability with the formula and the exclusions printed beside it, so that the number is something I can check rather than something I am told.

## UX flow (LabelDesk words)
1. **Trust → `Availability`** (public `/trust#availability`) — the current month, the coverage line "Measured from 41,200 samples since 3 November 2026", the formula in one sentence ("minutes a component was not operational ÷ minutes it was sampled, per component, then the worst component") and the exclusion list (planned maintenance announced on the status page, and windows with no samples). [empty: "No measured history yet — sampling began on <date>; a figure appears once 30 days are covered."] [loading: the panel renders skeleton rows and no number] [error: "The availability figure could not be loaded." with no placeholder percentage]
2. **A month with partial coverage** — the panel prints "Measured for 18 of 31 days" and shows no percentage, because a partial month is not a figure.
3. **A month with full coverage** — the percentage, the sample count, the worst component's name and the number of incidents that overlapped the month, each linked to its published incident.
4. **No number without samples** — a deployment that has never sampled reads exactly like the empty state; nothing on this panel defaults to 100 %.
5. **Flag off** — the section is absent from the trust page.

## Backend changes
- Models/schema: none — reads `StatusSample` from T-772 and published incidents from T-773.
- Routers: `app/routers/sla.py` (new) — `GET /api/public/sla?month=YYYY-MM` returns `{month, coverage_days, sampled_from, formula, exclusions, measured: null | {uptime, samples, worst_component, incidents}}`; `GET /api/ws/{ws}/sla` returns the same platform figure plus this workspace's incident ids inside the window.
- Services/jobs: `app/services/uptime.py` (new) — `MIN_COVERAGE_DAYS = 30`, `month_window(month) -> (since, until)`, `coverage(db, since, until) -> int` (distinct sampled days), `measure(db, since, until) -> dict | None` (`None` under `MIN_COVERAGE_DAYS`; otherwise per-component uptime from `StatusSample`, worst component, and overlapping published incidents), `exclusions() -> list[str]`, `FORMULA` constant.
- Permissions + workspace isolation: the public payload carries no tenant data; the workspace route filters incidents by `workspace_id == c.ws.id` and needs `workspace.read`.
- Secrets/safefetch: none.
- OpenAPI: yes.

## Tests to write first
pytest — `apps/backend/tests/test_uptime.py`:
- `test_under_thirty_days_of_samples_has_no_figure` — samples for 12 days → `GET /api/public/sla` returns `measured: null` and a coverage line naming 12 days.
- `test_thirty_days_produces_a_figure_from_the_samples` — 30 days of operational samples with one degraded day of 1440 minutes at 50 % → the figure equals the arithmetic and names the worst component.
- `test_a_component_with_no_samples_lowers_coverage_not_uptime` — `gpu` sampled on 10 of 30 days → the measured block's `samples` counts only what exists and the payload carries `coverage_gaps: ["gpu"]`.
- `test_the_formula_and_exclusions_are_returned_verbatim` — the payload's `formula` and `exclusions` equal the module constants.
- `test_published_incidents_in_the_window_are_listed` — one published and one unpublished incident in the month → only the published id is listed.
- `test_a_foreign_incident_is_not_in_the_workspace_payload` — workspace B's incident never appears in workspace A's `GET /sla`.
- `test_flag_off_hides_both_routes` — with the flag off → both routes 404.

node — `apps/frontend/tests/uptime-view.test.mjs`:
- `test_a_null_measurement_renders_the_empty_sentence` — `uptimeLine(null)` is the "No measured history yet" sentence, never "100%".
- `test_a_partial_month_shows_no_percentage` — `uptimeLine({coverage_days: 18, month_days: 31})` contains "18 of 31" and no `%`.
- `test_the_formula_is_one_sentence` — `formulaLine()` contains "not operational" and ends with one full stop.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The panel prints a figure only when the month has at least 30 sampled days and states the formula and exclusions verbatim.
- [ ] A month with partial coverage prints coverage instead of a percentage.
- [ ] A workspace sees only its own incidents in the workspace payload.
- [ ] Empty, loading and error states above all render.
- [ ] Workspace A cannot see or change workspace B's data (404, not 403, for cross-workspace ids).
- [ ] Flag off ⇒ no Availability section, both routes 404, and the trust page is otherwise unchanged.

## Notes for the implementer
Owner-gated: the target percentage, the measurement window and whether maintenance is excluded (INDEX §4.3). Until the owner sets a target this panel reports measurement only — no target line, no percentage in a heading, no green badge. A partial month is not extrapolated: "18 of 31 days" is the honest string and the reason the code returns `None` rather than a computed guess.
