## User story
As a reviewer, I want to see "100 images at 70/20/10 with 3× ≈ 210/20/10" before I press Create, so that nobody expects the validation split to grow and nobody picks a multiplier their plan refuses.

## UX flow (LabelDesk words)
1. **Wizard → step 5 — Output** — a multiplier select limited to the plan (`1× … N×`), the estimate block, and the note. [empty: n/a] [loading: the estimate line reads "calculating…"] [error: "Your plan allows up to 5× — upgrade or lower the multiplier." when the value arrives above the cap]
2. **Estimate block** — three numbers side by side (`train / valid / test`) with the sentence: "100 source images at 70/20/10 with 3× ≈ 210 / 20 / 10 — the split grows in train only."
3. **Realised-count note** — "The realised count can be lower: duplicate outputs are dropped and filters can remove images." with the numbers from preview when they are known.
4. **1×** — the select's help text: "1× keeps the original image only; augmentations replace it."
5. **Name field** — optional, placeholder `v{n}`, 120 characters.
