## User story
As the engineer sweeping a stubborn class, I want to write the space as a form, see what it will cost, and watch the trials land with their fingerprints, so that tuning is a decision I can afford rather than a script I have to write.

## UX flow (LabelDesk words)
1. **Train** (`/:ws/:project/train`) — a `One run` / `Sweep` toggle sits above the parameters; `Sweep` swaps the three fields for rows of min / max / list / fixed per parameter. [empty: a new space starts with one row and the sentence "A sweep needs at least one parameter with more than one value." until it is true]
2. **Estimate** — as the space changes, a block prints `About {n} trials, {cost} credits`; a space over 20 trials disables the start and names the cap. [loading: the estimate recomputes per keystroke with a quiet spinner, never a layout jump]
3. **Metric and budget** — one metric picker (`mAP50-95`, `recall@0.5`) and a budget in trials or minutes; the cheaper pre-flight trial is offered first with a checkbox.
4. **Start** — the dialog closes and the sweep opens on Insights → Runs filtered to its trials, each row carrying its parameter fingerprint and a `Pruned` badge when stopped early. [error: the budget runs out ⇒ the sweep header reads "The budget ran out; the best trial was kept." and the best row is marked]
5. **Make this the run** — promotes one trial; the header names it and the other rows stay for comparison. [loading: the promote button shows pending and the ledger refreshes]

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The Train screen toggles between one run and a sweep without losing the chosen version or architecture.
- [ ] The space is written as rows of min / max / list / fixed, and the estimate and the price update as it changes.
- [ ] Starting a sweep queues the trials and lands on their rows, each with its settings and a pruned badge where it stopped early.
- [ ] Empty, loading and error states above all render: a single-value space explains itself, the estimate shows pending, an exhausted budget names the kept trial.
- [ ] Workspace A cannot see or change workspace B's data (404, not 403, for cross-workspace ids).
- [ ] Flag off ⇒ the Train screen keeps today's three numbers and no sweep surface exists.
