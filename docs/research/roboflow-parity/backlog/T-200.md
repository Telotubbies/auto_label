## User story
As an inspection engineer, I want to pick a contrast algorithm instead of a yes/no switch, so that a low-contrast backlit frame gains detail without amplifying noise everywhere.

## UX flow (LabelDesk words)
1. **Wizard → step 3 → Contrast card** — a select with *Off*, *Contrast stretch (2nd–98th percentile)*, *Histogram equalisation*, *Adaptive equalisation (local tiles)* and a hint under each: "flattens haze", "flattens the whole frame", "local detail; can amplify noise". [empty: Off] [loading: n/a] [error: "That algorithm is not available." when an old recipe carries an unknown string]
2. **Preview** — the sample panel redraws; the card notes "labels unchanged".
3. **Legacy recipe** — a version generated with `contrast: true` displays as *Contrast stretch* on reuse.

## Tests to write first
pytest — `apps/backend/tests/test_contrast_modes.py`:
- `test_adaptive_raises_local_contrast` — a fixture with a dark left half and a bright right half: the standard deviation inside each half rises after `adaptive`, while the global mean stays within ±5%.
- `test_equalize_flattens_the_histogram` — the output histogram's largest bin count is lower than the input's.
- `test_stretch_maps_to_the_percentiles` — a frame with 1% saturated pixels at both ends is not crushed to pure black/white at the 2nd/98th percentile.
- `test_labels_are_untouched` — annotations identical before/after all three modes.
- `test_boolean_true_still_means_stretch` — a legacy `contrast: True` recipe equals `"stretch"` byte-for-byte.
- `test_unknown_algorithm_is_a_400_when_the_flag_is_on` — `contrast: "clarendon"` → `contrast_unknown`; with the flag off → `contrast_unavailable`.

## Notes for the implementer
CLAHE on a 3-channel image converts to LAB, equalises L, converts back — on a 12 MP image that is ~0.2 s, acceptable once per version build. Guard the tiles grid to `tileGridSize=(8,8)` and `clipLimit=2.0` so the preview and the build cannot diverge.
