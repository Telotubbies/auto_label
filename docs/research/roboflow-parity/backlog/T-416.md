## User story
As an owner, I want to stop paying and know exactly what survives, so that the decision is not a support call.

## UX flow (LabelDesk words)
1. **Plan & billing → `Cancel plan`** — a confirm screen (not a small dialog): `Cancel your plan?` with a
   `What happens next` list:
   `You keep access until 30 Nov 2026.` / `Then this workspace moves to Free: your projects stay private, the
   included allowance drops to the Free allowance.` / `Unused included credits are not refunded.` /
   `Prepaid credits stay until they expire.` / `Work stops when the Free allowance runs out.`
   Actions `Keep the plan` / `Cancel at period end`.
2. **After confirming** — the success screen: `Your plan ends on 30 Nov 2026. Nothing changes before then.` with
   `Reactivate` (clears `cancels_at`) and `Back to billing`.
3. **`[loading]`** the confirm button reads `Cancelling…`; the plan card behind shows `Ends 30 Nov 2026`.
4. **`[error]`** `not_configured` under the mock reads `No payment provider is connected, so the plan cannot be
   cancelled here.` and the confirm button is disabled with that reason; the Free plan offers no cancel at all
   (`You are on Free.`).
5. `[empty]` no active paid plan → the card shows the Free state and this screen is unreachable.

## Backend changes
- Models/schema: none (`BillingAccount.cancels_at`).
- Routers: `POST /api/ws/{ws}/billing/cancel` (T-413) is called with `{at_period_end: true}`;
  `POST /api/ws/{ws}/billing/reactivate` → clears `cancels_at`; both 409 for Free.
- Services/jobs: `services/billing.py` — `cancel(db, ws, at_period_end, actor)` and `reactivate(db, ws, actor)`; a
  worker tick that flips `plan` to `free` when `cancels_at` has passed and audits `billing.downgrade`. Nothing
  deletes data on downgrade; limits tighten on the next metered action (existing `metering.check`).
- Permissions + workspace isolation: `workspace.billing`; the account row is the workspace's.
- Secrets/safefetch: none.
- OpenAPI: yes.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Cancelling states the end date and the five consequences before it applies.
- [ ] A cancelled plan keeps access until the date, then downgrades to Free without deleting data.
- [ ] Reactivation clears the end date and is audited.
- [ ] Empty, loading and error states above all render.
- [ ] Workspace A cannot see or change workspace B's data (404, not 403, for cross-workspace ids).
- [ ] Flag off ⇒ no cancel link and no sub-route.

## Notes for the implementer
One provider call, one local state change. The five consequences are copy, not policy: do not invent a refund path
and do not delete anything on downgrade. Owner decisions (provider, ladder, self-host licence) remain open.
