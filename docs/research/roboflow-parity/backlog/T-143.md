## User story
As a reviewer, I want one sheet that carries the instructions, the people and the batch's current state, so that assigning and later re-dealing the work does not need two different screens.

## UX flow (LabelDesk words)
1. **Annotate board → To label → Assign** — the sheet names the batch and its image count, shows the instruction textarea (prefilled from the batch), and a member list with checkboxes (the current assignee pre-ticked). Assign is disabled until at least one person is ticked. [loading: the batch's instructions are fetched with the card read, so the sheet never opens with an empty textarea that then fills]
2. **Assign** — sends the instructions and the assignees in one call; the sheet closes and the board shows one card per person. Toast: "7 images to Somchai and Nok". [error: 400 for a non-member or an oversized note → the field or the row carries the message, the sheet stays open]
3. **Manage** — the same sheet opens from an Annotating or Review card with a different footer: current counts ("2 of 7 labelled · 5 remaining"), the instructions (editable), the assignee list with a **Reassign** action, and two guarded bulk actions: **Send 5 to unannotated** and **Add 2 to the dataset** where the number is what the action will actually move. [empty: a card with nothing labelled disables "Send to unannotated"]
4. **Guard** — both bulk actions require a confirm line naming the count; adding to the dataset also names the classes involved (T-129's summary).
5. **Delivery honesty** — the assigned people see the card on their board; the sheet says "They will see it in Annotate", never "we emailed them", because delivery is area 7's sink.
6. **Keyboard** — the member list is a checkbox group; the instruction field is a textarea with a character counter; the footer's primary is the last tab stop.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Assign names the batch and count, carries the instructions, and sends instructions plus assignees in one request.
- [ ] The board shows one card per assignee after a multi-assign and marks the viewer's own card.
- [ ] Manage mode shows labelled/remaining counts, allows reassignment, and guards both bulk actions with their real counts.
- [ ] The sheet never claims a notification method that does not exist.
- [ ] Empty, loading and error states above all render.
- [ ] Workspace A cannot see or change workspace B's data (404, not 403, for cross-workspace ids) — the routes are T-142's.
- [ ] No flag: replaces the existing modal.
- [ ] Keyboard-only path: board → Assign → Tab the member checkboxes → Space → Tab to Assign → Enter.

## Notes for the implementer
One component, two modes, keyed by `mode: "assign" | "manage"` — two nearly identical sheets would drift on the instruction field. "They will see it in Annotate" is the honest sentence for a workspace with no notification sink configured.
