## User story
As an owner facing a launch week, I want to buy a block of credits at my own rate, so that a spike does not hit the
overage rate or stop work.

## UX flow (LabelDesk words)
1. **Plan & billing → `Prepaid credits`** — a card with the current balance
   (`12.5 prepaid credits · expires 4 Feb 2027`), the workspace's per-credit price and its volume discount, a
   quantity stepper (`10`, `50`, `100`, `Custom`), the computed total, and `Buy credits`.
2. **While no price is configured** — the card reads `Prepaid credits are not configured for this workspace.` and
   `Buy credits` is disabled with that reason; the balance still shows.
3. **Confirm** — `Buy 50 credits for …` with the explicit total (`Pricing not set` blocks the step); after the mock
   checkout the card shows the new balance and `Expires 12 months after purchase.`
4. `[empty]` balance zero: `No prepaid credits. They are spent after the included allowance and expire one year
   after purchase.` `[loading]` a skeleton on the balance line. `[error]` a declined charge leaves the balance
   untouched and says `Payment declined — no credits were added.`
5. **Spend order** — stated under the balance: `Included credits are spent first, then prepaid, then on-demand (if
   enabled).`

## Backend changes
- Models/schema: `BillingAccount.prepaid_credits` exists; add `prepaid_expires: FLOAT | None` to the same model and
  one audit-backed ledger row per purchase — no new table: a purchase writes an `Audit` row `billing.prepaid` with
  `{credits, expires}` and increments `prepaid_credits`.
- Routers: `POST /api/ws/{ws}/billing/prepaid` `{credits}` → `{balance, expires, checkout_url}`; `409 not_configured`
  while the price is unset; `400` for `credits <= 0` or above a configured maximum.
- Services/jobs: `services/billing.py` — `buy_prepaid(db, ws, credits, actor)` (checkout via the provider, credit only
  on `checkout.completed`), `prepaid_balance(db, ws) -> dict` (expired balance reads zero), and
  `deps.charge` consumes prepaid after the included allowance in one place.
- Permissions + workspace isolation: `workspace.billing`; the balance is the workspace's row.
- Secrets/safefetch: none; the provider call is the mock.
- OpenAPI: yes.

## Frontend changes

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A prepaid purchase is only credited after the provider confirms, and the balance expires after a year.
- [ ] Spending order is included → prepaid → on-demand, enforced in one place.
- [ ] No price is invented: an unset price disables the purchase with a reason.
- [ ] Empty, loading and error states above all render.
- [ ] Workspace A cannot see or change workspace B's data (404, not 403, for cross-workspace ids).
- [ ] Flag off ⇒ no card, no route, no charge change.

## Notes for the implementer
The purchase ledger is an audit row, not a table — the balance plus one audit row is enough to reconcile, and the
audit log already answers "who bought what". Owner decisions (provider, ladder/prices, self-host licence) are open.
