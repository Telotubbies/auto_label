## User story
As someone who has never trained a model, I want one button and one sentence of explanation, so that my first result arrives without a form I do not understand.

## UX flow (LabelDesk words)
1. **Models → empty state** — primary *Train a model*, secondary *Get a quick model*. The secondary carries one line: "Fixed short recipe · free · detection only".
2. **Confirm dialog** — "What this does" lists: 20 epochs at 640 px, YOLO26 Nano, no augmentation, up to 1,000 images; "What it costs" says "No credits. It uses GPU time like any run."; primary *Start quick model*, secondary *Cancel*. [loading: the primary reads "Starting…"; the dialog cannot be dismissed by a backdrop click]
3. **Refusals in the dialog** — under 20 approved images: the primary is disabled and the sentence states the count; over 1,000: disabled with a *Go to Train* link; classification project: the dialog is not reachable (the entry is absent).
4. **After start** — the dialog closes, the toast says "Quick model queued — watch the job tray.", and the list shows the queued row immediately.
5. **On the quick model's card** — the badge *Quick model*, T-275's note, and a confidence hint: "Quick models are usually reliable at a threshold around 0.85–0.99 — check the evaluation before deploying." shown once, with a link to the evaluation. No preprocessing or augmentation rows appear for a quick model anywhere in Train (they are fixed and not editable).
6. **Train page for a quick project** — *Train a model* still works normally; the quick model is never a template for it.

- **States** — [empty: a classification project shows no entry at all] [error: a 402 renders inside the dialog with the plan and the remedy].
