## User story
As a reviewer, I want one event on screen at a time with one key per decision, so that thirty questionable frames take minutes instead of an afternoon.

## UX flow (LabelDesk words)
1. **Stream detail → Events → "Review marked events"** — opens the session dialogue: how many events match (`34 marked wrong or unclear`), the model to improve, and Start. If someone else holds it, the dialogue shows their name, the start time, the item count and "Check again" instead of Start. [empty: `Nothing marked wrong or unclear yet`] [loading: `Starting…` over a skeleton frame] [error: a 409 shows the holder; a 403 says `You need review permission in this project`]
2. **The session** — a full-width card: the event image on the left, the model's boxes and the event's verdict on the right, and a footer bar with `Add to dataset (1)`, `Send to annotation (2)`, `Reject (3)`, `Snooze (4)`, plus progress `7 of 34` and `Save & exit`.
3. **Keyboard** — `1`–`4` decide, `S` snoozes, `Esc` asks "Save and exit?" before leaving; nothing decides on a bare Enter, so a stray keypress cannot route an image.
4. **After the last item** — the card becomes a summary: how many went to the dataset, to annotation, were rejected or snoozed, with "Back to events" and "Start another session".
5. **Expiry** — `[error: a decision after the lock expired]` an inline bar says "This session expired. Your last decision was not saved." with "Restart", and the decisions already made stay made.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Starting a session shows one event at a time with the four actions and progress; the last item gives a summary.
- [ ] `1`–`4` decide, typing in a field never decides, and `Esc` confirms before leaving.
- [ ] A blocked or expired session names the holder or the expiry and offers the next step, without losing decisions already made.
- [ ] Empty, loading and error states above all render.
- [ ] Workspace A cannot see or change workspace B's data (404, not 403, for cross-workspace ids).
- [ ] No flag: with `task_340` off the button and dialogue are absent.
