## User story
As a labeler, I want one bar that always says where I am, which workspace I am in and what is running, so that the loudest button on the screen stops inviting me to skip labelling.

## UX flow (LabelDesk words)
1. **Any screen** — a single 56 px bar: workspace switcher, breadcrumb (`Projects / PPE Detection / Annotate`), `⌘K Search`, `Jobs 2`, theme toggle, account menu. Height is identical on every route, including the editor. [empty: a workspace with one workspace shows the switcher without a chevron menu]
2. **Switcher** — the only place a workspace changes; it lists `/me` workspaces with a check on the current one and a `Create workspace` row. Choosing one keeps the same page where the slug exists, else lands on Projects.
3. **Jobs** — the icon shows a count of active jobs and opens the tray (T-481); a finished job shows a dot, not a badge that never clears.
4. **No Train button anywhere in the chrome** — on Upload, Annotate, Dataset, Versions, Models and Deploy the page header owns the primary. [error: with no project context the bar renders the workspace switcher, `⌘K` and the account menu only]
5. **Offline** — a banner inside the bar says "You are offline — actions are paused" and every primary is disabled with a reason; the bar keeps its height.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] One bar at 56 px on every route, including the editor and 375 px (mobile keeps its menu button).
- [ ] The workspace switcher is reachable in exactly one place and lists only the caller's workspaces.
- [ ] `Jobs` shows the active count and opens the tray; `⌘K` opens the palette; the account menu holds Account and Sign out.
- [ ] No `Train Model` renders outside the Train stage.
- [ ] Empty, loading and error states above all render: while `/me` loads the bar renders its skeleton at the same height; a failed jobs call shows `Jobs` with no count; offline shows the banner and disables the primaries.
- [ ] Workspace A cannot see workspace B's data: the switcher lists only `me.workspaces`, and a hand-typed slug for a foreign workspace 404s in `WorkspaceGate`.
- [ ] Flag off ⇒ the floating 44 px bar, the sidebar switcher and the two portal buttons render unchanged.

## Notes for the implementer
Delete the portal rather than hide it: `#topbar-actions` was the mechanism that made Train the global primary, and leaving the element in place invites the next card to reuse it. The bar is full width, not an inset pill — that is what makes its height constant above scrolling content.
