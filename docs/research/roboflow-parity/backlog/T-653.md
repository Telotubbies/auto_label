## User story
As a labeler opening today's active-learning batch, I want to see why each image is in my queue and how much of today's cap is gone, so that I am not re-labelling at random and my manager can point at the same numbers.

## UX flow (LabelDesk words)
1. **Annotate board → Active learning card** (`/:ws/:project/annotate`) — the card shows the queue state: "18 of 25 used today", the remaining count and a "Plan today's picks" button. [empty: cap reached ⇒ "Today's cap is used — it resets at midnight" with the used/limit line] [loading: "Scoring 200 candidates…" with the estimate "about 200 model calls"]
2. **The plan** — three fields: how many picks, the per-class floor, the exploration share; a sentence states the cost before anything runs. [error: "no ready model — train one first" with a link to Train]
3. **The picks** — a list, one row per pick: image thumbnail, class, reason ("low margin 0.04", "rare class: harness", "new cluster", "exploration") and score, sortable by score.
4. **After training** — a second line on the same card: "32 of 40 picks are now recognised" or "not measured yet — train a model on this batch".
5. **Flag off** — no card, and the board is exactly as today.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The card states used, limit and remaining for today and refuses to plan when the cap is spent.
- [ ] Every pick shows its reason and score, and the plan states the model-call estimate before the run.
- [ ] A finished batch later reports how many picks the retrained model recognises, or says it is not measured yet.
- [ ] Empty, loading and error states above all render, including no ready model and a zero-candidate pool.
- [ ] Workspace A cannot see workspace B's queue: both calls are project-scoped and the card never renders without a project.
- [ ] Flag off ⇒ no card and no summary request on the board.

## Notes for the implementer
The reason is shown verbatim from the server, never re-worded in the client, so two screens cannot disagree. Do not colour the score: a colour ramp here would imply a calibrated probability, and the queue is ranked, not scored. The rules form for enabling active learning belongs to parity `6-F6.19` (area 6) — this card only adds the queue, the reasons and the budget line.
