## User story
As a reviewer, I want every number in the health report to be a doorway into the images behind it, so that
the report ends in a fixable list instead of a dashboard to admire.

## UX flow (LabelDesk words)
1. **Dataset · Health** — header stats: image count, annotation count, average image size, median aspect
   ratio, images with no annotations. [empty: no images → "No images yet — upload 20 images or use the demo
   set", one primary] [loading: five stat skeletons]
2. **Dataset · Health · Annotation heat map** — the 10 × 10 map with a legend; dragging a rectangle
   highlights it and the caption updates "412 annotations in 3 × 2 cells"; releasing opens the grid with
   the region chip (T-102). A caption explains what it detects: "Boxes concentrated in one part of the frame
   usually mean a fixed camera and a cropping problem."
3. **Dataset · Health · Objects per image** — a bar histogram (0, 1, 2 … 20+); clicking a bar opens the grid
   filtered to that count range; the caption says why it matters ("no images with 0 objects — add
   negatives").
4. **Dataset · Health · Image dimensions** — raw sizes and aspect-ratio buckets as two compact bars, with
   the note "Resizing happens in versions, not here." [error: analytics fails → the cards show "—" with one
   Retry, the rest of the page still renders]
5. **Dataset · Health** — each chart's click sets an explainable chip on the grid (`Annotation region`,
   `0 annotations`, `3–5 annotations`), never an unexplained filter.
