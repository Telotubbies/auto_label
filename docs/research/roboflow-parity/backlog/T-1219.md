## User story
As the design and quality leads, I want one audit of the test-first deploy page, the playground, the stream
detail and the workflow builder, so that "ship it" and "watch the line" are judged on what a person can
read and do, at a desk and in the field.

## UX flow (LabelDesk words)
1. **Deploy & Test** (`/deploy?tab=test`) — the sample, the boxes, the threshold, the endpoint card, the
   wrapped snippet, the cold-start sentence.
2. **API tab** — the snippet tabs, the key note, the error-code list and the credit rule.
3. **Streams tab and detail** (`?tab=streams`, `/streams/:sid`) — the list states, the live view, the zones,
   the rules and the event timeline.
4. **Playground** (`/:ws/playground`) — up to three models side by side, latency, the VLM question, the
   disabled states with reasons.
5. **Workflows** (`task_31`) — the block chain, the required ports first, the published inputs.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The test pane leads; a sample works before any code is read, and the snippet wraps at 1440 and 375.
- [ ] The copy button never covers the first line (the A11 contract).
- [ ] A stream's state words (`connecting`, `live`, `reconnecting`, `stopped`) are distinct and explained;
      a dead source is not a frozen image.
- [ ] Playground lists a model once per family group; disabled models state the reason (the A21 contract).
- [ ] axe: 0 critical/serious; the MJPEG image has an accessible name; no colour-only state.
- [ ] The 10 heuristics are scored per screen; no score < 3; the two worst are named.
- [ ] Findings are S1–S4 with evidence; each S1/S2 has a proposed fix card title.
- [ ] P1 and P4 walk the area; SUS-8 and any dead end are recorded.
- [ ] Empty, loading and error states: no deployed model, no cameras, a 503 predict with a retry, a
      flag-off surface with a sentence and a way back.

## Notes for the implementer
The live view is a fixture frame; do not judge stream smoothness here (that is the perf card and the real
GPU run). Judge whether the screen explains what is happening.
