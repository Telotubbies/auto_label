## User story
As someone choosing a model to compare, I want the list to tell me what differs between rows, so that I am
not reading the same vendor word ten times to find the one thing that changes.

## UX flow (LabelDesk words)
1. **Playground** (`/:ws/playground`) → Models card — rows group under their family (`YOLO26`, `YOLO11`,
   `YOLOv8`), each group headed once with the vendor in muted text at `text-xs`. Each row keeps its name
   and its checkbox.
2. **Selection** — the `0 / 3` counter and the three-model cap are unchanged. [empty: unchanged] [loading:
   unchanged] [error: a family with no models renders no group]

## Notes for the implementer
Options considered:
1. **Group by family with one heading (recommended).** Ten rows become three short groups, and the word
   that never changes stops competing with the word that does.
2. **Drop the vendor column entirely.** Rejected: the vendor is the reason the licence badges differ, so it
   has to stay somewhere.
3. **Keep it per row at 12 px.** Cheapest, but it keeps ten identical strings on a list whose job is
   comparison.

The group heading is the only place the vendor may appear. Do not add a second sort order; the registry
already sorts by family then size.
