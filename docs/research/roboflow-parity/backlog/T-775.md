## User story
As the customer's finance lead, I want to file the claim in the product with the month and the incident ids already filled in, so that a missed month is a form with a record instead of an argument by email.

## UX flow (LabelDesk words)
1. **Trust → Availability → `Claim a service credit`** (public `/trust`, and signed in at `/:ws/legal/trust`) — with no published credit schedule the button is present and disabled, reading "No credit schedule has been published yet." and the panel shows the measured month beside it. [loading: the card renders its skeleton and no button]
2. **A published schedule and a missed month** — the card reads "November 2026: 99.42 % — you may claim the published credit for this band" with `File a claim`; the band name comes from the schedule, never from this code.
3. **The claim form** — month (fixed), the incident ids in the window (pre-filled, read-only), invoice or PO number (required), contact address (pre-filled from the account), optional note. `File` stays disabled until the invoice number is present. [error: "The claim window has closed — claims must be filed within one billing cycle." with the closing date]
4. **Filed** — "Claim CL-<id> filed. The credit appears on a future invoice once approved." and one audit row with the filer and the month.
5. **An operator approves** (`/:ws/admin`) — the claim list shows month, measured figure, band, invoice and state; `Approve` writes a credit-note stub with a number and an approver, `Reject` requires a reason. [empty: "No claims filed yet."]

## Backend changes
- Models/schema: `SlaClaim(id, workspace_id, month, measured, band, invoice_ref, contact, note, state, filed_by, filed_at, decided_by, decided_at, reason, credit_note_no)` appended to `app/models/incidents.py`.
- Routers: `app/routers/sla.py` — `GET /api/ws/{ws}/sla/claimable?month=` (`null` when the schedule is empty or coverage is short), `POST /api/ws/{ws}/sla/claims` (201 with `CL-` reference; 409 outside the one-cycle window; 422 without `invoice_ref`), `GET /api/ws/{ws}/sla/claims` (this workspace), plus operator routes `GET /api/sla/claims` and `PATCH /api/sla/claims/{id}` behind `require_superadmin`.
- Services/jobs: `app/services/sla_claims.py` (new) — `SCHEDULE: list[dict] = []` (band name, floor, credit percentage — empty until the owner publishes one), `band_for(measured) -> dict | None`, `claimable(db, ws, month) -> dict | None`, `window_closes(month) -> float` (the last day of the following month), `file(db, c, body) -> SlaClaim`, `decide(db, claim, state, approver, reason) -> str` (credit-note number from `new_id()`).
- Permissions + workspace isolation: filing needs `workspace.billing`; a workspace reads only its own claims; the operator list is superadmin only. The invoice reference is never returned to another workspace.
- Secrets/safefetch: none — no card data is stored, only the invoice reference the customer typed.
- OpenAPI: yes.

## Tests to write first
pytest — `apps/backend/tests/test_sla_claims.py`:
- `test_no_schedule_means_no_claim` — `SCHEDULE` empty → `GET …/sla/claimable` returns `null` and `POST …/claims` returns 409 with the "no credit schedule" copy.
- `test_a_schedule_and_a_missed_month_offers_the_band` — monkeypatch `SCHEDULE` with one band and a measured month under it → `claimable` names the band, the month and the closing date, and carries no percentage as a bare number.
- `test_the_window_is_one_billing_cycle` — a claim for a month two cycles old → 409 with the closing date that has passed.
- `test_an_invoice_reference_is_required` — `POST …/claims` with `invoice_ref=""` → 422; with one → 201 and a `CL-` reference.
- `test_a_labeler_cannot_file_a_claim` — labeler → 403; billing role → 201.
- `test_approval_writes_a_credit_note_and_an_audit_row` — superadmin `PATCH` `{state: "approved"}` → `credit_note_no` set, `decided_by` recorded, one `sla.claim.decide` audit row; `rejected` without a reason → 422.
- `test_a_workspace_sees_only_its_claims` — claims filed by two workspaces → each `GET` lists one.
- `test_flag_off_hides_the_claim_routes` — with the flag off → every route on this card 404.

node — `apps/frontend/tests/credit-claim.test.mjs`:
- `test_without_a_schedule_the_button_says_so` — `claimState(null)` is `{disabled: true, line: "No credit schedule has been published yet."}`.
- `test_a_claim_needs_an_invoice_number` — `canFile({invoice_ref: ' '})` is false, `('INV-2026-11')` is true.
- `test_a_closed_window_names_the_date` — `windowLine('2026-09', now)` contains the closing day and the word "closed".

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] With no published schedule the claim is visible, disabled and explains why — it never invents a percentage.
- [ ] A filed claim stores month, measured figure, band, invoice reference and filer, and is listed once.
- [ ] Approval writes a credit-note number and an approver; rejection needs a reason; both are audited.
- [ ] Empty, loading and error states above all render.
- [ ] Workspace A cannot see or change workspace B's data (404, not 403, for cross-workspace ids).
- [ ] Flag off ⇒ no claim card, no operator list, all routes 404, and the Usage tab is unchanged.

## Notes for the implementer
Owner-gated: the credit bands, their percentages, the claim window and who approves (INDEX §4.3) — `SCHEDULE` ships empty and every test that exercises a band monkeypatches it, so no number can leak into the product by default. The credit note is a stub with a number and an approver: it is not an invoice and must never be described as money already credited.
