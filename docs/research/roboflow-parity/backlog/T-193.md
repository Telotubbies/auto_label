## User story
As a reviewer, I want the wizard to mark a contradictory recipe before I press Create, so that I never lose a round trip — and never see a message the server would word differently.

## UX flow (LabelDesk words)
1. **Wizard → any step** — a conflicting field shows an inline message under the control, and the *Create version* button is disabled with "Fix 2 conflicts to continue". [empty: n/a] [loading: n/a] [error: the message is the copy table's, keyed by the server's code]
2. **Wizard → step strip** — a step holding a conflict gets a dot; clicking the dot jumps to that step. [loading: n/a] [error: n/a]
3. **Wizard → footer** — the conflicts summary collapses to one line: "Tiling cannot be combined with crop." with a "Show both" expander when there are two.
