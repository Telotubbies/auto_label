## User story
As a reviewer of a long clip, I want a list of tracks with their span and score so that I can accept or delete the object's life in the clip instead of scrubbing to frame 412 of 1,800.

## UX flow (LabelDesk words)
1. **Video → Track a concept** (`/:ws/:project/videos`) — a prompt field, an optional "use the selected box as a seed", a start frame and a direction toggle. [empty: no video selected yet ⇒ "Pick a video to track"; with a video but no frames, the button is disabled with "Frames are still being extracted"]
2. **Run** — `[loading: "Tracking 30 frames…" with the existing job polling]`, `[error: "SAM 3.1 is not available on this worker" or the job's own error]`.
3. **The track list** — a `Card` beside the timeline: id chip, class, first and last frame, length, mean score, an "interpolated" badge for frames the tracker wrote. A row click jumps to the first frame; hovering highlights that track on the current frame.
4. **Actions per track** — "Delete track" (with the count of frames it will remove), "Re-run from this frame", "Extend backwards". Deleting asks once and names the number.
5. **The unconfirmed count** — a footer line: "12 frames in this clip hold no track" so an incomplete pass is visible.
6. **Flag off** — `task_22` off means the whole page is absent, as today.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The panel starts a track from a prompt or a seed box, with a direction and a start frame, and shows progress while it runs.
- [ ] The list groups annotations into tracks with span, length, mean score and an interpolated badge, and one click jumps to a track's first frame.
- [ ] A track is deleted as a unit after a confirmation that names the frame count, and the unconfirmed frames are counted.
- [ ] Empty, loading and error states above all render, including no video, extracting frames and an unavailable model.
- [ ] Workspace A cannot see workspace B's tracks: every row comes from this video's timeline response.
- [ ] Flag off (`task_22`) ⇒ no page, so the panel cannot render.

## Notes for the implementer
The unit is the track, not the frame: never offer a per-frame delete in this panel. Mean score is the arithmetic mean of the frames the tracker wrote, labelled as such; a keyframe's human score is not averaged in. "Extend backwards" is the same call with `direction: "backward"`, not a second mechanism.
