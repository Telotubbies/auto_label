## User story
As a customer's finance contact, I want to ask for an invoice, see what is owed and pay it by transfer from one list, so that a purchase order and a tax invoice stop needing three emails.

## UX flow (LabelDesk words)
1. **Settings → Plan & Usage → `Invoices`** (`/:ws/settings/usage`) — a card below the shipped meters. [loading: three skeleton rows] [empty: "No invoices yet. Request one when your finance team needs a tax invoice." / "ยังไม่มีใบแจ้งหนี้ ขอได้เมื่อฝ่ายการเงินต้องการใบกำกับภาษี"] [error: "Could not load invoices." + **Try again**]
2. **`Request invoice`** — a `Modal`: company name, tax id, address, PO number, billing email, a withholding-note field, and one read-only line `"Amount: Pricing not set"` where the plan price would be. Submitting with the amount unset is refused by the server (`pricing_not_set`) and the sheet prints `"Pricing is not set on this deployment yet — nothing is charged."` / `"ยังไม่ได้กำหนดราคาบนระบบนี้ ยังไม่มีการเรียกเก็บเงิน"` Nothing is sent anywhere.
3. **A row** — `INV-2569-0001 (draft) · issued 17 Nov 2026 · due 24 Nov 2026 · ฿12,345.60 · Draft`, with `Open document`, `Payment details`, `Credit note`. The status chip reads `Draft`, `Awaiting payment`, `Overdue 30 days`, `Credited` through `chipTone`.
4. **`Payment details`** — a `Modal` with the QR at 160 px on `Card`, the reference in monospace with **Copy reference**, the masked account, the amount and `"The invoice is marked paid by our finance team once the transfer lands. It stays awaiting payment until then."` When the deployment has no PromptPay id: `"PromptPay is not configured on this deployment. Pay by bank transfer using the reference below."` [error: `promptpay_expired` prints `"This QR expired — regenerate it before paying."` with **Regenerate**]
5. **`Credit note`** — amount and reason, then a confirm; the row gains `-฿… credited` beside the total and the credit note appears as its own row.
6. **Overdue** — a `Card` banner above the list: `"Invoice INV-2569-0001 is 30 days overdue. New jobs are not paused."` and, under the row, `"Reminders sent: 7 days, 14 days, 30 days after due."` No card payment, no auto-charge, no provider name anywhere.

## Backend changes
- Models/schema: none — T-755 and T-757 own every column and route.
- Routers: none.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The card lists invoices with number, dates, total and status, and an empty state before the first one.
- [ ] `Request invoice` sends company, tax id, address, PO, email and withholding note; with pricing unset the refusal is explained and nothing is charged.
- [ ] `Payment details` shows the QR, the reference and the masked account, or the not-configured sentence; an expired QR offers regenerate.
- [ ] A credit note reduces the row and appears as its own line; the overdue banner and reminder line state the facts without pausing anything.
- [ ] Empty, loading and error states above all render: no invoices, the skeleton, the load error with **Try again**, and `pricing_not_set` inline.
- [ ] Workspace A cannot see or change workspace B's data (404, not 403, for cross-workspace ids) — a 404 refreshes the list.
- [ ] Flag off ⇒ no Invoices card, no request, and the usage page is exactly today's.

## Notes for the implementer
Owner-gated: who may issue an invoice, what the day-30 end state is, and whether a card option exists at all — ship "no card payment on this deployment" until the owner rules, and never print a price we do not have (INDEX §4.3). `"Pricing not set"` is the only string allowed in a money position: a `0.00` reads as a free plan and a blank reads as a bug. The tax invoice is the accountant's document, not a receipt: this screen links to it, and must not re-render totals in a second format that can drift from T-756's satang math.
