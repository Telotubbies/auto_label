## User story
As a supervisor on a phone, I want four thumb-reachable tabs and a vertical pipeline, so that I can check what is waiting without pinch-zooming a desktop layout.

## UX flow (LabelDesk words)
1. **375 × 812, any workspace screen** — a glass bottom bar with `Projects · Work · Models · More`, 48 px targets, safe-area padding; the top bar keeps the switcher and the job button, and the hamburger drawer is gone. [empty: `Work` with nothing queued opens the caught-up panel]
2. **Home** — title, the single next task as a full-width card, then project cards stacked; no two-column KPI grid.
3. **Project overview** — a vertical stepper: five rows with dot, count and one action; no horizontal card strip, no horizontal scroller anywhere on the page.
4. **`More`** — a sheet with Settings, Activity, Members (role-gated), the theme toggle and Sign out.
5. **Landscape 812 × 375 and 200% zoom** — the tabs stay, the content scrolls vertically, and nothing is clipped. [error: a route that does not exist under a tab shows the not-found panel inside the tab shell, not a blank screen]
