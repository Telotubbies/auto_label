## User story
As an engineer with a colour-shifting camera, I want hue rotation, sensor-gain variation and directional motion blur, so that the model stops keying on one lens and one exposure.

## UX flow (LabelDesk words)
1. **Wizard → step 4 → Add augmentation → Hue / Camera gain / Motion blur** — bounded sliders only. [empty: n/a] [loading: n/a] [error: "Length must be 0–100 px." inline]
2. **Camera gain** — one percent field described as "sensor amplification — adds noise and brightness together"; the hint is the difference from *Brightness*: "Brightness scales the image; gain mimics the sensor."
3. **Motion blur** — *Length* and *Angle*; the preview shows a directional smear instead of an even one. [empty: angle 0 = horizontal]
4. **Grayscale conflict** — if grayscale preprocessing is on, hue and saturation cards show "Colour changes do nothing after grayscale." (a warning, not an error).

## Backend changes
- Models/schema: none.
- Routers: none.
- Services/jobs: `app/services/imaging.py` — `hue` (rotate the H channel by `±degrees` in HSV via `numpy`, wrap at 1.0), `gain` (multiply then clip with a random `N(1, pct)` multiplier per image, plus a small gaussian noise term so gain is not just brightness), `motion_blur` (build a 1-D kernel of `length` at `angle` and convolve with `ImageFilter.Kernel` on a padded copy, or a Pillow box-blur rotated via transpose when the angle is a multiple of 90). All three leave labels untouched.

## Notes for the implementer
Do the HSV route with `numpy` on a uint8 array and convert back once — per-pixel `colorsys` on 12 MP is minutes. Motion blur length 0 must be a no-op, not a 1-px kernel (`ImageFilter.Kernel` rejects even sizes; pad the kernel with zeros).
