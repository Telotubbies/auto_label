## User story
As a video reviewer, I want to see what the model marks in one 30-second range before spending on a whole batch, so that "annotate 40 clips" is a decision made on evidence.

## UX flow (LabelDesk words)
1. **Project → Video → Auto label** (header button, `project.review` only) — the dialog lists the project's ready videos with checkboxes (the current batch pre-ticked), and the action rows: a class select plus a one-line description of when the action happens ("when the worker lifts the box"). [empty: no ready videos → "Videos are still being prepared" with the extraction progress] [error: a video longer than 30 minutes is listed with "Too long — 31 min" and cannot be ticked]
2. **Sample rate** — a slider `0.5–12` fps, default 8, with the line "8 frames a second catches short actions and costs more" and a live estimate: `40 clips × 2 min × 8 fps ≈ 38,400 AI calls`.
3. **Preview a range** — a compact strip of the first ticked video; dragging selects up to 30 seconds (the drag clamps at 30 s and says so). **Run preview** costs the frames in that range and returns proposed segments drawn on the strip with their class colours and `frames_seen`. Nothing is saved. [loading: "Looking at 240 frames…"] [error: 402 → the allowance sentence and a Usage link; 400 → the field-level reason]
4. **Annotate all videos** — opens a confirm naming videos, actions, fps, credits and "existing segments of these classes are replaced"; Confirm queues the job and closes, the job tray shows it, and the Actions review list (T-148) receives the segments. [error: the queue call fails → the dialog stays open with the server's message]
5. **What replaces what** — the confirm repeats the replace rule in plain words: "Segments of helmet and vest in these 40 clips will be replaced. Other classes are untouched."
6. **Keyboard** — the range selection is reachable without a pointer: the strip is a slider with `←`/`→` moving the in point and `Shift`+arrows the out point, each step 0.5 s.
