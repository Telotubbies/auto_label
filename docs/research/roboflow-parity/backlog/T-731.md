## User story
As a CCTV operator, I want the camera's privacy switches and the neighbour's window mask on the page I already edit, with a preview of what the public sees, so that redaction is a setting rather than a promise in a policy document.

## UX flow (LabelDesk words)
1. **`ความเป็นส่วนตัว`** — a section on the camera's detail page: two switches (`เบลอใบหน้า`, `เบลอป้ายทะเบียน`), a mask list and an export policy. [empty: a camera with no masks reads `ยังไม่มีพื้นที่ปิดทึบ` with the draw action] [loading: `กำลังโหลดนโยบาย`] [error: `โหลดนโยบายไม่สำเร็จ — ลองอีกครั้ง`]
2. **The preview** — two panes side by side, `ตัวอย่างที่สาธารณะเห็น` and `ภาพต้นฉบับ`, each on a solid well, with what was applied printed under them (`ใบหน้า 2 · ป้ายทะเบียน 1 · พื้นที่ 1`). [error: `ยังไม่มีภาพให้ตรวจ — เปิดกล้องก่อน`]
3. **A switch the box cannot honour** — turning on `เบลอป้ายทะเบียน` with no plate detector shows `เปิดเบลอป้ายทะเบียนไว้แต่ยังไม่ได้ติดตั้งตัวตรวจจับ — กล้องนี้ไม่รับภาพเข้าชุดข้อมูล` and saves the policy with the warning, because the operator must know the camera is now refusing frames. [error: that sentence]
4. **Masks** — `วาดพื้นที่ปิดทึบ` reuses the shipped polygon editor in mask mode; a saved mask appears in the list with its point count and a delete. [error: `บันทึกพื้นที่ไม่สำเร็จ — ลองอีกครั้ง`]
5. **The export policy** — a two-option control whose `ส่งออกต้นฉบับ` option is dimmed with the lock line for a non-manager; changing it to `original` asks for a standing reason and states that every package records it. [error: `ส่งออกต้นฉบับต้องมีสิทธิ์ผู้จัดการและเหตุผลที่บันทึกไว้`]

## Backend changes
- Models/schema: none.
- Routers: none — the section reads the stream payload and patches the three fields through `PATCH /streams/{sid}`, and previews through `GET /streams/{sid}/privacy-preview`.
- Services/jobs: none.
- Permissions + workspace isolation: the page is `<PermGate perm="project.streams">`; the switches and masks are disabled unless `can('project.privacy')` and the policy unless `can('project.settings')`; the API enforces both and a foreign stream id is 404 through T-728.
- Secrets/safefetch: none.
- OpenAPI: no — no route changes.

## Frontend changes
- Route: none — a section inside the shipped stream detail page.
- Nav entry: none.
- Components:
  - `apps/frontend/src/features/streams/PrivacySection.tsx` (new) — the switches, the mask list, the preview panes and the export policy.
  - `apps/frontend/src/features/streams/privacyView.ts` (new, pure) — `appliedLine(applied)`, `maskRows(masks)`, `policyOptions(can)`, `plateWarning(available)`.
  - `apps/frontend/src/features/streams/ZoneEditor.tsx` — one `mode="mask"` prop so masks are drawn with the editor that already exists instead of a second polygon tool.
  - `apps/frontend/src/features/streams/StreamDetail.tsx` — one `<PrivacySection stream={stream} />` below the zones section.
  - `apps/frontend/src/lib/i18n.ts` — the keys below.
- API client: `apps/frontend/src/lib/api/privacy.ts` (new) — `privacyPreview(ws, p, sid)`, `savePrivacyPolicy(ws, p, sid, body)`.
- Glass: the two preview panes are solid `.canvas-well` panes inside a `Card`; the switches are the shipped `Switch` rows; the policy is the shipped `Tabs` in two segments; nothing here is glass. No new token, no hex, `text-sm`/`text-xs` only, `lucide-react` only.
- Feature flag: `task_redaction`; off ⇒ no section, no masks read and the camera page is exactly today's.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The camera page carries both switches, the mask list and the export policy, and a saved change survives a reload.
- [ ] The preview shows the redacted and the original frame with the counts of what was applied.
- [ ] A policy the box cannot honour states the refusal before the camera silently drops frames.
- [ ] Empty, loading and error states above all render.
- [ ] Workspace A cannot see or change workspace B's camera policy (404, not 403).
- [ ] Flag off ⇒ no section and the camera page is unchanged.
