## User story
As a customer's finance team paying by transfer, I want a QR for the exact baht amount and one unique reference on the invoice, so that the payment matches itself and nobody has to email a screenshot.

## UX flow (LabelDesk words)
No screen in this card (T-758 renders it):
1. **Payment instructions** — `GET /api/ws/{ws}/billing/invoices/{id}/payment` answers `{configured: false}` on a fresh install, or `{promptpay: {target_masked: "081-xxx-4471", payload, qr_svg}, bank: {name, account_masked, account_name}, reference: "LD-2569-0001", amount_satang, expires}`. Amount and reference are derived from the invoice, never typed.
2. **Not configured** — the workbench prints `"PromptPay is not configured on this deployment. Pay by bank transfer using the reference below."` The invoice stays `draft` and no account number is invented. [empty: that sentence, never a blank QR box]
3. **Expired QR** — after `expires` the route answers 410 `{"code": "promptpay_expired"}` and the screen offers `Regenerate`; regenerating mints a new reference and audits it.
4. **Payment arrives** — nothing in this card marks it paid: matching a transfer is a finance action, and the invoice stays `"awaiting payment"` until a person records it (`POST /billing/invoices/{id}/paid` is deliberately *not* in this card — see Notes).
5. **Reminders** — 7, 14 and 30 days past `due_at` the worker mails the billing address once per threshold with the amount, the reference and `"Reference LD-2569-0001 — 12,345.60 baht, due 24 Nov 2026."`; the invoice is never silently cancelled. [error: a mail failure is recorded on the row and retried on the next tick]
6. **An unpaid invoice at day 30** — the row carries `"overdue"` and the workspace banner reads `"Invoice INV-2569-0001 is 30 days overdue. New jobs are not paused."` — because the pause policy is not decided yet.

## Backend changes
- Models/schema: `BillingInvoice` gains `reminders JSON DEFAULT '[]'` (the thresholds already sent) and `reference VARCHAR(64) DEFAULT ''`; one block appended to `app/schema/billing.py` (T-755 owns that file; merge after it). No other table.
- Routers: in `app/routers/billing.py` — `GET /billing/invoices/{id}/payment` (200 with instructions, `configured: false` when the deployment has no PromptPay id, 410 `promptpay_expired` when the reference is past its window) and `POST /billing/invoices/{id}/payment/reference` (regenerates the reference, audits `billing.reference`).
- Services/jobs: `app/services/promptpay.py` (new) — `taf(target)`, `payload(target, amount_satang, ref) -> str` (EMVCo TLV: `00 02 01`, `29` with the PromptPay AID `A000000677010111`, the target, `54` amount, `58` country `TH`, `62` the reference, `63` CRC16-CCITT over the payload), `crc16(data) -> str`, `qr_svg(payload) -> str` (the shipped `segno` dependency), `mask_target(target)`. `app/services/invoicing.py` gains `instructions(db, ws, invoice) -> dict`, `REMIND_DAYS = (7, 14, 30)`, `enqueue_reminders(db)` (one `invoice_remind` job per tick when any invoice crossed a threshold) and `run_reminders(job)` (mails through the shipped `mailer.send`, appends the threshold to `reminders`, audits `billing.remind`, never changes the amount). `invoice_remind` joins `LOCAL_JOB_KINDS`; the worker loop calls `invoicing.enqueue_reminders()` beside `retention.enqueue_if_due()`.
- Permissions + workspace isolation: `workspace.billing` on both routes; the invoice resolves inside `c.ws.id`, so a foreign id is 404; the PromptPay target and bank account come from `config` and are masked in every response (only the last four digits are returned).
- Secrets/safefetch: the PromptPay id and bank account are configuration, not secrets, and are never returned unmasked; no provider is called, no network request leaves the process, and `BillingProvider` (T-412) stays the mock.
- OpenAPI: yes — two routes and the instructions shape.

## Tests to write first
pytest — `apps/backend/tests/test_promptpay_dunning.py`:
- `test_the_payload_carries_an_amount_a_country_and_a_valid_crc` — `payload("0812345678", 1234560, "LD-1")` contains `54061234` (amount `1234.56`), `5802TH`, and its last four characters equal `crc16(payload[:-4])`.
- `test_a_thai_mobile_target_is_normalised_to_the_international_form` — `payload("081-234-5678", …)` carries `0066812345678` in tag 29, not the dash form.
- `test_the_qr_is_an_svg_without_a_network_call` — `qr_svg(payload)` starts with `<svg` and monkeypatching `socket.socket` to raise does not fail.
- `test_an_unconfigured_deployment_returns_configured_false_and_no_account` — with empty config the route answers `{configured: false}`, no payload and no account digits.
- `test_an_expired_reference_is_410_and_regenerate_mints_a_new_one` — the route 410s with `promptpay_expired`; `POST …/reference` answers a different reference and audits `billing.reference`.
- `test_reminders_fire_once_per_threshold` — an invoice `due_at` 8 days ago sends one mail and appends `7` to `reminders`; a second run sends nothing; at 15 days it sends again with `14`.
- `test_reminders_never_change_amount_or_status` — after three thresholds the totals are unchanged and the status is still `draft`/`awaiting payment`.
- `test_foreign_invoice_and_flag_off` — 404 for another workspace's invoice; with the flag off both routes are 404 and no reminder job exists.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Instructions carry a valid PromptPay payload, an SVG QR, a unique reference and the exact invoice amount, or `configured: false` with no invented number.
- [ ] The PromptPay target and bank account are masked in every response; nothing unmasked leaves the API.
- [ ] Reminders fire once at 7, 14 and 30 days past due, name the amount and the reference, and never change the invoice.
- [ ] Empty, loading and error states above all render: not configured, `promptpay_expired` with a regenerate action, and a mail failure recorded on the row.
- [ ] Workspace A cannot see or change workspace B's data (404, not 403, for cross-workspace ids).
- [ ] Flag off ⇒ no instructions, no QR, no reminder job and no mail.

## Notes for the implementer
Owner-gated: whose PromptPay id and whose bank account receive the money, what the invoice end state is at day 30 (pause new jobs, read-only, or cancel), and whether a person or a bank feed marks an invoice paid — none of it is an engineer's call, so `ENFORCEMENT = "none"` ships and the banner says new jobs are not paused (INDEX §4.3). PromptPay is invoice-first by construction: there is no reusable mandate, so a "subscription" is a new invoice each cycle and `trigger`-style recurring charges must not be offered. Record payment by hand for now — do not add a route that flips an invoice to paid from a client call, and do not parse a bank feed in this card. The QR encoding and the reminder thresholds are conventions quoted from the group file; a Thai accountant confirms both before a customer sees an invoice (BEYOND §5).
