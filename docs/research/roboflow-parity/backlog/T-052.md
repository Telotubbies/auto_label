## Notes for the implementer
Each deletion must stand alone in the diff with one line of reason in the commit message; a reviewer
should be able to reject one and keep the other seven. `Invite.invited_by` and the duplicate
`event_search_frames` index are **not** in this card — dropping a column or an index needs a DDL path this
repo does not have. If a `grep` shows a symbol you are about to delete is live, stop and report: the audit
claim is then wrong, and the report matters more than the deletion.
