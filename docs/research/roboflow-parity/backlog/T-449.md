## User story
As an agency owner, I want to compare my client workspaces side by side, so that I can see which one is behind or
overspending before a monthly call.

## UX flow (LabelDesk words)
1. **Workspace switcher → `Organization overview`** (visible only when the caller belongs to an organization) →
   `/orgs/:org`. Header: the organization name, a `This cycle` / `Last 30 days` / `Custom` range, and
   `3 workspaces · 1 not shown`.
2. **Table** — one row per workspace: `Workspace`, `Plan`, `Members`, `Projects`, `Images`, `Deployments`, `Credits`,
   `Storage`, `Last activity`, and a row action `Open`. Sortable on credits and last activity; the totals row is pinned
   under the table.
3. **Comparison strip** — three small figures above the table: `Total credits`, `Busiest workspace`, `Quietest workspace`
   (by last activity), each one line.
4. **`[empty]`** no linked workspace the caller can see: `No workspaces to compare yet. Link a workspace in Settings →
   Workspace.` with a link to the current workspace's settings.
5. **`[loading]`** a five-column skeleton table; **`[error]`** `Could not load the organization overview.` + `Try again`;
   a `404` renders `This organization is not available to your account.` without a retry loop.
6. **Mobile** — the table collapses to cards of `name · plan · credits`, one per workspace.
