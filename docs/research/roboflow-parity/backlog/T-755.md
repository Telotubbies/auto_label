## User story
As a customer's finance team, I want a baht invoice with a separate 7% VAT line and our tax id on it, so that we can expense LabelDesk against a purchase order instead of arguing about a card subscription in dollars.

## UX flow (LabelDesk words)
No screen in this card (T-758 owns the list and the request form):
1. **A request** — `POST /api/ws/{ws}/billing/invoices {buyer_name, buyer_tax_id, buyer_address, po_number, billing_email, lines: [{description, quantity, unit_amount_satang}]}` answers `{id, number: "INV-2569-0001 (draft)", subtotal_satang, vat_satang, total_satang, status: "draft", dry_run: true}`.
2. **No configured price** — with `pricing.py` still `None` and no `lines`, the route answers 422 `{"code": "pricing_not_set"}` and the screen prints `"Pricing not set"` where the amount would be. [empty: an invoice with no lines shows `"Pricing not set"` in every money column, never `0.00`]
3. **The VAT line** — `7%` of the subtotal, computed on satang integers and rounded half-up once at the end, so the invoice total always equals `subtotal + vat` to the satang.
4. **A credit note** — `POST …/{id}/credit-note {amount_satang, reason}` answers a new row of `kind: "credit_note"` pointing at the invoice, with its own `number` preview and the same VAT split. A credit larger than the invoice is 422.
5. **The number is a draft** — every `number` returned today ends in `(draft)` and the row keeps `dry_run: true`; the screen never prints it as an issued tax-invoice number.
6. **A foreign invoice id** — 404 on read and on credit note.

## Backend changes
- Models/schema: twelve columns added to the shipped `BillingInvoice` (T-412) — `currency VARCHAR(3) DEFAULT 'THB'`, `subtotal_satang INTEGER DEFAULT 0`, `vat_satang INTEGER DEFAULT 0`, `total_satang INTEGER DEFAULT 0`, `vat_rate FLOAT DEFAULT 7.0`, `buyer_name VARCHAR(255) DEFAULT ''`, `buyer_tax_id VARCHAR(32) DEFAULT ''`, `buyer_address TEXT DEFAULT ''`, `po_number VARCHAR(64) DEFAULT ''`, `due_at FLOAT`, `paid_at FLOAT`, `payment_ref VARCHAR(64) DEFAULT ''`, `kind VARCHAR(16) DEFAULT 'invoice'`, `parent_id VARCHAR(32)`, `dry_run BOOLEAN DEFAULT TRUE`. One `ADD COLUMN` block in `app/schema/billing.py` (new), registered as `("billing_invoices", billing.BILLING_INVOICES)` in `schema.STEPS`.
- Routers: `app/routers/billing.py` (new), prefix `/api/ws/{ws}`, `dependencies=[Depends(require_flag("task_thai_invoicing"))]` — `GET /billing/invoices` (newest first, both kinds), `POST /billing/invoices`, `GET /billing/invoices/{id}`, `POST /billing/invoices/{id}/credit-note`. Money is integer satang in every request and response; 422 for a negative amount, an unknown id, a credit above the invoice, or `pricing_not_set`.
- Services/jobs: `app/services/invoicing.py` (new) — `VAT_RATE = 7.0`, `vat_of(subtotal_satang) -> int` (`(subtotal * 700 + 5000) // 10000`, half-up once), `totals(lines) -> (subtotal, vat, total)`, `number_preview(db, ws, at) -> str` (year in the workspace's era, `INV-<year>-0001 (draft)`, one per workspace, never reused), `create(db, ws, actor, body) -> BillingInvoice`, `credit_note(db, ws, invoice, amount_satang, reason) -> BillingInvoice`, `out(invoice) -> dict`. The plan amounts come from `pricing.pack_ladder()`; while every price is `None` the service refuses to invent one.
- Permissions + workspace isolation: `c.perm("workspace.billing")` on read and create (owner/billing), `workspace.update` on a credit note; every row is resolved by `workspace_id == c.ws.id`, so a foreign invoice is 404.
- Secrets/safefetch: none — no payment provider call in this card and no card data stored; `buyer_tax_id` is a company number, not personal data.
- OpenAPI: yes — four routes, the invoice shape and the 422 `pricing_not_set` code.

## Tests to write first
pytest — `apps/backend/tests/test_thai_invoice.py`:
- `test_vat_is_seven_percent_and_rounds_half_up_once` — `vat_of(10000)` is 700; `vat_of(10050)` is 704 (703.5 rounds up); `vat_of(1)` is 0; `total == subtotal + vat` for 1,000 random subtotals.
- `test_a_draft_invoice_splits_vat_and_keeps_satang` — `POST` with one line of 12,345,600 satang answers `subtotal 12345600`, `vat 864192`, `total 13209792`, `currency "THB"`, `dry_run true`.
- `test_no_price_means_pricing_not_set` — `POST` with no lines and an unconfigured `pricing.py` is 422, `code == "pricing_not_set"`, and no row is written.
- `test_the_number_is_a_draft_preview_and_never_repeats` — two invoices answer two different previews, both ending in `(draft)`, and neither is stored as an issued number.
- `test_a_credit_note_points_at_its_invoice` — a 5,000,000-satang credit produces `kind: "credit_note"`, `parent_id == invoice.id`, its own VAT split, and leaves the invoice total unchanged.
- `test_a_credit_above_the_invoice_is_422` — one satang over the total is refused and no row is written.
- `test_flag_off_and_foreign_invoice` — with `task_thai_invoicing` off all four routes are 404; an invoice id from another workspace is 404.
- `test_billing_permission_is_required` — a labeler is 403 on create and read, and a member with the `billing` role is allowed.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] An invoice stores buyer name, tax id, address, PO, billing email and integer-satang lines, and returns a 7% VAT split that always sums to the total.
- [ ] A credit note is a second row pointing at its invoice, with its own VAT split, and cannot exceed the invoice.
- [ ] Every number returned is marked `(draft)`; nothing claims to be an issued tax-invoice number.
- [ ] Empty, loading and error states above all render: no lines ⇒ `pricing_not_set`, the 422 copy, and an empty list.
- [ ] Workspace A cannot see or change workspace B's data (404, not 403, for cross-workspace ids).
- [ ] Flag off ⇒ no invoice routes, no row and no money anywhere; the plan page still says to contact the operator.

## Notes for the implementer
Owner-gated: the legal entity, tax id, registered address and VAT registration of the seller, whether we are the VAT registrant, and which invoice series the accountant wants — none of it is an engineer's choice (INDEX §4.3). The document must carry the Revenue Department's invoice elements — the words "tax invoice", seller name/address/tax id, buyer name/address, a serial number, description/quantity/value per line, the VAT amount separately, and the issue date — as a *specification to be confirmed by an accountant*; numbering, withholding-tax treatment and e-tax filing are unverified here (BEYOND §5). Charge nothing: no provider is called and `pricing.py` stays `None`.
