## User story
As a Thai systems integrator, I want one login that lists my customer workspaces, allocates licences from the pool I bought and prints a monthly statement, so that I can resell LabelDesk without handling a single customer API key.

## UX flow (LabelDesk words)
1. **`พาร์ทเนอร์`** (`/:ws/partner`) — the customer list with licences used against the pool and the data region per customer. [empty: `ยังไม่มีลูกค้า — สร้างเวิร์กสเปซลูกค้ารายแรก`] [loading: `กำลังโหลดรายชื่อลูกค้า`] [error: `โหลดรายชื่อลูกค้าไม่สำเร็จ — ลองอีกครั้ง`]
2. **Create a customer** — name, data region and a licence quantity; the child workspace is created with the partner as its owner and the region the customer asked for. [error: `โควต้าหมด — ขอเพิ่มก่อนจัดสรร` when the pool cannot cover it, and nothing is created]
3. **Allocate** — `จัดสรรใบอนุญาต` moves licences from the pool to one customer, and the pool line updates `ใช้ไป 42 จาก 60`. [error: that depletion sentence with the remaining number]
4. **The statement** — a month picker and a table: customer, licences used, overage, amount. Every amount reads `ยังไม่กำหนดราคา` because no price exists yet, and the CSV download carries the same word. [empty: `ยังไม่มีการใช้งานในเดือนนี้`] [error: `สร้างใบแจ้งยอดไม่สำเร็จ — ลองอีกครั้ง`]
5. **What a partner cannot see** — opening any customer's project, image or stream URL from this login is 404: the console reads aggregates only, and support access stays a separate, consented, time-boxed feature. [error: `ไม่พบข้อมูล — พาร์ทเนอร์เห็นเฉพาะสรุปการใช้งาน`]

## Backend changes
- Models/schema: `app/models/partner.py` (new) — `PartnerAccount(id, user_id, name, status, created)` and `LicenceGrant(id, partner_id, workspace_id, quantity, kind, period_start, period_end, created)`; `Workspace` gains `parent_id VARCHAR(32)` (null) and `partner_id VARCHAR(32)` (null) — one `WORKSPACES` block in `app/schema/identity.py`; register the module in `models/__init__.py`.
- Routers: `app/routers/partner.py` (new), prefix `/api/partner`, `dependencies=[Depends(require_flag("task_partner_console"))]`; `GET "/customers"`, `POST "/customers"` `{name, region, quantity}` → 201 or 409 `licence pool exhausted`, `POST "/grants"` `{workspace_id, quantity}` (409 when the pool cannot cover), `GET "/statement?month="` → `{rows: [{workspace, licences_used, overage, amount, pricing}], pool}`, `GET "/statement.csv"` through `services/csvsafe`. 403 for a caller with no `PartnerAccount`, 404 for a child that is not this partner's.
- Services/jobs: `app/services/partner.py` (new) — `pool(partner) -> {bought, allocated, free}`, `licences_used(db, workspace)` (streams attached to a station, the unit the group file prices), `create_child(db, partner, body, user_id)` writing the workspace through the existing `routers/workspaces.create` path, `allocate`, `statement(db, partner, month)` with `amount: None` and `pricing: "not_set"`. No job, no charge, no provider.
- Permissions + workspace isolation: the console route resolves a `PartnerAccount` from the caller and accepts no workspace id except a child's; a child of another partner is 404. The partner's own workspace membership grants nothing inside a child workspace — this is the one deliberate cross-workspace read in the product and it is limited to counts, never to project routes.
- Secrets/safefetch: `services/secretbox.py` — no credential is stored for a partner; `services/safefetch.py` — not called, and the statement is a local CSV.
- OpenAPI: yes — five routes and their schemas.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A partner sees only its own child workspaces with their licence use, region and pool, and creates a child with a region and a quantity.
- [ ] Allocation beyond the pool is refused with the number and creates nothing.
- [ ] The monthly statement lists usage and overage, every amount reads `ยังไม่กำหนดราคา`, and the CSV says the same.
- [ ] Empty, loading and error states above all render.
- [ ] Workspace A cannot see or change workspace B's data: a partner login cannot open a customer's project, image or stream (404, not 403, for a customer workspace's ids), and a child of another partner is 404.
- [ ] Flag off ⇒ no route, no nav item, no partner row read, and workspace creation is unchanged.

## Notes for the implementer
Owner-gated: whether an SI buys a licence pool, at what unit (per camera or per project) and what an overage costs (INDEX §4.3, decision 3). Until the owner answers, a statement prints `ยังไม่กำหนดราคา` and nothing is charged. R7: this is the only login that reads across workspaces, so the surface is aggregates only — licence counts, plan and region — and every project, image, stream and package route keeps its own tenancy check, which is what makes the 404 in the test suite the real guard. Stored and how long: the partner account, its grants and the child link are kept for the life of the contract; the statement is computed from live counts on each call, so there is no second ledger to disagree with reality. Failure direction: a pool that cannot cover an allocation refuses it; a partner whose account is disabled gets 403 and sees no customer list, never a stale one. Support access with the customer's consent is deliberately **not** built here — it is a consented, time-boxed read and belongs with the tenancy work T-430 owns.
