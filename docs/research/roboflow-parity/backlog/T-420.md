## User story
As an owner, I want to add non-admin seats and self-serve SSO to a cheap workspace, and to see exactly what turns off
if I stop paying, so that the trade is visible before I buy.

## UX flow (LabelDesk words)
1. **Plan & billing → Add-ons** — the `Teams` row: `Non-admin seats for labelers and reviewers, plus single sign-on.`
   with `Add Teams` and a pre-flight line `0 of 10 non-admin seats used.` or
   `12 non-admins on this workspace — add Teams to raise the cap, or make some members admins first.`
2. **Add** — while the price is unset the button is disabled with `Pricing not set — ask your LabelDesk operator`;
   with a test price it runs the mock checkout and returns to Plan & billing with `Teams is active.`
3. **Cancel Teams** — confirm `Cancel Teams? It stays on until 30 Nov 2026.` On expiry the row states the full
   consequence list: `Every member becomes an admin.` / `Single sign-on turns off; sign-in falls back to emailed
   links.` / `Review mode, labelling analytics and workflow version history turn off.`
4. **`[error]`** more than the cap allows → `More than 10 non-admin members — make some admins or remove them first.`
   shown before any checkout, and again as a `409` if the count changed meanwhile.
5. **`[empty]`** the add-on is already active → `Active until 30 Nov 2026` with `Cancel Teams` as the only action.
6. **Seat rule** — an invite above the cap answers `402 Non-admin seats are full — add Teams or make a member an admin.`

## Backend changes
- Models/schema: none new — `BillingAccount` records the add-on through a `WorkspaceFlag` row (`addon_teams`) plus
  the account's `plan_code`; `PLANS[...]["seats"]` stays the source for admin seats.
- Routers: `POST /api/ws/{ws}/billing/addons/teams` `{action: "add"|"cancel"}` → `{active, ends_at, non_admin_used,
  non_admin_cap}`; `GET` the same shape inside `/billing`.
- Services/jobs: `services/billing.py` — `teams_state(db, ws)`, `add_teams(db, ws, actor)` (pre-flight count, mock
  checkout, `409 over_cap`), `cancel_teams(db, ws, actor)`, `expire_addons(db, now)` (worker tick): on expiry every
  `Member` becomes `admin`, `WorkspaceFlag(addon_teams)` off, SSO disabled, and the three surfaces gated by the
  entitlement map (area 1's `T-042`) turn off.
- Permissions + workspace isolation: `workspace.billing` to change; the seat count and the expiry are the workspace's.
- Secrets/safefetch: none.
- OpenAPI: yes.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The add-on shows its seat rule and refuses a purchase that exceeds the cap before any checkout.
- [ ] Cancel keeps the add-on until the period end, and the expiry consequences are listed before opting in.
- [ ] On expiry every member becomes an admin, SSO turns off and the gated surfaces turn off.
- [ ] Empty, loading and error states above all render.
- [ ] Workspace A cannot see or change workspace B's data (404, not 403, for cross-workspace ids).
- [ ] Flag off ⇒ no add-on row, no cap change, invite behaviour unchanged.

## Notes for the implementer
The cap is a count of non-admin members, not of all members. Price stays `None` until the owner decides the ladder —
the pre-flight rule ships now because it is policy, not pricing.
