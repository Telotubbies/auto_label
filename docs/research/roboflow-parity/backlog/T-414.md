## User story
As an owner, I want my plan, the pack ladder and the add-ons on one page, so that "what am I on and what would more
cost" is one screen.

## UX flow (LabelDesk words)
1. **Settings → Plan & billing** (`/:ws/settings/billing`) — plan card: tier label, cycle, `Renews on …` or
   `Ends on …` when cancelling, and `Cancel plan` (T-416).
2. **Pack ladder** — a vertical list, one row per pack: `+N credits / month`, the amount, and `per credit`. While a
   price is unset the row reads `Pricing not set — ask your LabelDesk operator` and its `Choose` button is disabled
   with that reason. The Free row is the baseline (`Included on your plan`) and packs add on top.
3. **Add-ons** — one row per add-on (`Teams`), with `Add`/`Remove` and its own unset-price state.
4. **`Go to portal`** and the invoice list live in T-415's card, linked from the plan card's footer.
5. **`[empty]`** on Free with no packs configured: the ladder shows the baseline row and the sentence
   `No packs are configured for this workspace.`; the primary action is `Talk to us about a plan` (mailto to the
   operator, no checkout).
6. **`[loading]`** three skeleton rows for the plan card and the ladder; **`[error]`** `Could not load billing.` +
   `Try again`, and the page still shows the current plan label from the workspace payload.
7. **`409 not_configured`** on `Choose` → an inline line under the row (`This pack has no price yet.`), never a toast.

## Backend changes
- None (T-413 ships the payload). One shared line: `GET /api/ws/{ws}/billing` includes `plan.label` and
  `plan.included_credits` from `config.PLANS` so the page can show the baseline without a second call.

## Frontend changes
- Route: `/:ws/settings/billing` (new Settings tab). The old `Plan & Usage` tab keeps usage charts under
  `Usage & billing`; the plan card links both ways.
- Nav entry: the workspace rail's `Usage & billing` item points at `settings/usage`; the billing tab is reached from
  that page's header and the command palette (no seventh rail item).
- Components: `features/settings/billing/PlanCard.tsx` (new), `features/settings/billing/PackLadder.tsx` (new),
  `features/settings/billing/AddonList.tsx` (new), `features/settings/billing/packCopy.ts` (new) —
  `priceLine(pack)`, `perCredit(price, credits)`, `isConfigured(pack)`.
- API client: `billingApi` from T-413.
- Feature flag: `task_billing`; off ⇒ no tab, no rail link, no billing card.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The pack ladder shows credits, amount and per-credit rate, and refuses to invent a price.
- [ ] Add-ons are listed with their own add/remove and unset-price states.
- [ ] Empty, loading and error states above all render.
- [ ] Workspace A cannot see or change workspace B's data (404, not 403, for cross-workspace ids).
- [ ] Flag off ⇒ the tab and any billing card are absent; the usage page is unchanged.

## Notes for the implementer
Every number on this page comes from `/billing.catalogue`; a component that hard-codes a price fails review. Owner
decisions (provider, ladder/prices, self-host licence) are open — the unset-price row is the honest UI until then.
